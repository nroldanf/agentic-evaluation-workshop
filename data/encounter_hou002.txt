ENCOUNTER ID: HOU-002
LOCATION: The Houses of Healing, Minas Tirith
PATIENT: Frodo Baggins
EXAMINER: Aragorn
PRESENT: Gandalf, Samwise Gamgee, Meriadoc Brandybuck, Peregrin Took, Legolas, Gimli

---

FRODO: Gandalf?

GANDALF: I'm here.

FRODO: I thought you were dead.

GANDALF: So did I, for a while.

MERRY: He's awake!

PIPPIN: We thought you'd sleep forever.

FRODO: Careful, careful.

GIMLI: About time.

LEGOLAS: You look better than we expected, Master Baggins.

ARAGORN: Merry, Pippin, give me a moment with him. I want to examine him properly, now that he's truly awake.

PIPPIN: Fine. But we're coming back as soon as you're done.

ARAGORN: Frodo. Do you know where you are?

FRODO: You said... Minas Tirith. The Houses of Healing, I think.

ARAGORN: That's right. Do you know how long it's been since they brought you down from the mountain?

FRODO: I have no idea, honestly.

ARAGORN: Several days. You slept through most of it.

SAM: I slept a lot too, but not as much as you, Mr. Frodo.

ARAGORN: Let me see your hand.

FRODO: The finger's gone.

ARAGORN: I know. Sam told me what he could.

FRODO: Gollum bit it off. At the end, right before he fell with the Ring.

ARAGORN: It's healing well, considering everything. I'm going to clean it again and change the dressing. Tell me if it hurts.

FRODO: Ah — yes, a little, there.

ARAGORN: Almost done. How do you feel, in general?

FRODO: Tired. Very tired, still.

ARAGORN: And thirst? Hunger?

FRODO: Thirst, a lot. More than I've ever had in my life, though they've given me water since I woke up. Hunger not so much, honestly.

SAM: That's putting it mildly. He's barely wanted to eat anything.

FRODO: I don't have much appetite for that yet, Sam.

ARAGORN: Open your mouth for me a moment... yes, your mouth is dry. That fits with the thirst.

ARAGORN: Let me note your vitals. Temperature, thirty-seven degrees Celsius. Heart rate, seventy-eight beats per minute. Blood pressure, one hundred over sixty-eight millimeters of mercury. Oxygen saturation, ninety-seven percent.

SAM: Noted.

ARAGORN: I also see some minor burns here, on your hands, and a bit on your face. From the ash and heat of the mountain.

FRODO: I hadn't noticed those until you mentioned them.

ARAGORN: That's common, when the body was busy with more urgent things. They're not deep, but we'll keep tending them.

ARAGORN: And the shoulder? The old wound, from Weathertop.

FRODO: That doesn't hurt anymore. Hasn't for a while, honestly. I hadn't even thought about it until you mentioned it.

ARAGORN: Good — and it matches what I'm seeing. Nothing left there at all.

ARAGORN: Tell me, how have you been sleeping, in what you've been awake between sleeping?

FRODO: Not much, apart from that one long sleep. I wake up several times each night.

ARAGORN: Any particular reason? Pain, nightmares?

FRODO: It's hard to explain.

ARAGORN: Take your time.

FRODO: I still feel the weight sometimes. The Ring's, I mean. I know it's gone, that it was destroyed. But I still feel it, like it's still there.

ARAGORN: Since when do you feel that?

FRODO: I couldn't say an exact moment. It started somewhere along the road, long before we reached the mountain.

FRODO: And in some of those dreams, I feel like something's watching me. Like the Eye is still there. I still feel it a little when I wake up, even though I know the danger's passed.

ARAGORN: Have you talked to anyone else about this? To Sam?

FRODO: A little. I don't want him worrying more than he already is.

SAM: I worry either way, Mr. Frodo, whether you tell me or not.

ARAGORN: That sounds like a heavy toll to carry, even now that it's over. I'll make a note of it, so someone can talk this through with you properly, once you've recovered more from everything else. It's not something we need to resolve today.

FRODO: All right.

ARAGORN: For now, the most important thing is that you keep eating and resting. We'll see about the rest.

SAM: That's exactly what I've been telling him.

ARAGORN: Good. Then let's start there — Sam, can you get him something to eat, even if it's small?

SAM: Gladly, Mr. Frodo.

GIMLI: Can we all come back in now, or are you still playing healer?

ARAGORN: Almost done, Gimli. Just a moment more.
