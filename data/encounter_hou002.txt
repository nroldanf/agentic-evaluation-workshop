ENCOUNTER ID: HOU-002
LOCATION: The Houses of Healing, Minas Tirith
PATIENT: Frodo Baggins
EXAMINER: Aragorn
PRESENT: Gandalf, Samwise Gamgee, Meriadoc Brandybuck, Peregrin Took, Legolas, Gimli

---

FRODO: (waking slowly, in a bed, clear light coming through the window) Gandalf?

GANDALF: (at the foot of the bed, smiling) I'm here.

FRODO: I thought you were dead.

GANDALF: So did I, for a while.

(Merry and Pippin come running in and throw themselves onto the bed to hug Frodo)

MERRY: He's awake!

PIPPIN: We thought you'd sleep forever.

FRODO: (laughing, a bit overwhelmed) Careful, careful.

(Gimli and Legolas enter, followed by Aragorn)

GIMLI: About time.

LEGOLAS: You look better than we expected, Master Baggins.

(Sam appears last, in the doorway. Frodo sees him and the two of them just look at each other for a moment, saying
nothing)

ARAGORN: (approaching the bed) Merry, Pippin, give me a moment with him. I want to examine him properly, now that
he's truly awake.

PIPPIN: (leaving, not entirely convinced) Fine. But we're coming back as soon as you're done.

(Merry and Pippin leave. Gimli and Legolas stay near the door. Sam moves closer to the bed, on the other side from
Aragorn)

ARAGORN: Frodo. Do you know where you are?

FRODO: You said... Minas Tirith. The Houses of Healing, I think.

ARAGORN: That's right. Do you know how long it's been since they brought you down from the mountain?

FRODO: I have no idea, honestly.

ARAGORN: Several days. You slept through most of it.

SAM: I slept a lot too, but not as much as you, Mr. Frodo.

ARAGORN: Let me see your hand.

FRODO: (looking at it, as if seeing it clearly for the first time) The finger's gone.

ARAGORN: I know. Sam told me what he could.

FRODO: Gollum bit it off. At the end, right before he fell with the Ring.

ARAGORN: (examining the wound) It's healing well, considering everything. I'm going to clean it again and change the
dressing. Tell me if it hurts.

FRODO: (as Aragorn works) Ah — yes, a little, there.

ARAGORN: Almost done. How do you feel, in general?

FRODO: Tired. Very tired, still.

ARAGORN: And thirst? Hunger?

FRODO: Thirst, a lot. More than I've ever had in my life, though they've given me water since I woke up. Hunger not
so much, honestly.

SAM: (cutting in) That's putting it mildly. He's barely wanted to eat anything.

FRODO: I don't have much appetite for that yet, Sam.

ARAGORN: We'll work on that, little by little. I also see some minor burns here, on your hands, and a bit on your face.
From the ash and heat of the mountain.

FRODO: I hadn't noticed until you mentioned it too.

ARAGORN: That's common, when the body was busy with more urgent things. They're not deep, but we'll keep tending them.

ARAGORN: And the shoulder? The old wound, from Weathertop.

FRODO: (thinking it over) That doesn't hurt anymore. Hasn't for a while, honestly. I hadn't even thought about it until
you mentioned it.

ARAGORN: Good, that's what I was hoping to hear.

ARAGORN: (to Sam, finishing the physical exam) Note the vitals: temperature thirty-seven degrees. Pulse at seventy-eight.
Respiration sixteen. Blood pressure one hundred over sixty-eight. Oxygen saturation ninety-seven.
All within expected range, considering everything.

SAM: (writing it down) Noted.

ARAGORN: Tell me, how have you been sleeping, in what you've been awake between sleeping?

FRODO: Not much, apart from that one long sleep. I wake up several times each night.

ARAGORN: Any particular reason? Pain, nightmares?

FRODO: (hesitating) It's hard to explain.

ARAGORN: Take your time.

FRODO: I still feel the weight sometimes. The Ring's, I mean. I know it's gone, that it was destroyed. But I still
feel it, like it's still there.

ARAGORN: Since when do you feel that?

FRODO: I couldn't say an exact moment. It started somewhere along the road, long before we reached the mountain.

FRODO: And in some of those dreams, I feel like something's watching me. Like the Eye is still there. I still feel
it a little when I wake up, even though I know the danger's passed.

ARAGORN: Have you talked to anyone else about this? To Sam?

FRODO: A little. I don't want him worrying more than he already is.

SAM: (cutting in) I worry either way, Mr. Frodo, whether you tell me or not.

ARAGORN: I'll make a note of it, so someone can talk this through with you properly, once you've recovered more from
everything else. It's not something we need to resolve today.

FRODO: All right.

ARAGORN: For now, the most important thing is that you keep eating and resting. We'll see about the rest.

SAM: That's exactly what I've been telling him.

ARAGORN: Good. Then let's start there — Sam, can you get him something to eat, even if it's small?

SAM: (already standing up) Gladly, Mr. Frodo.

GIMLI: (from the doorway) Can we all come back in now, or are you still playing healer?

ARAGORN: (smiling) Almost done, Gimli. Just a moment more.

END OF TRANSCRIPT
