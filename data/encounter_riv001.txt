ENCOUNTER ID: RIV-001
LOCATION: The House of Elrond, Rivendell
PATIENT: Frodo Baggins
EXAMINER: Elrond
INFORMANT: Arwen (collateral historian; patient arrives unconscious)

---

ELROND: Carefully, here, on the table. Good. Arwen, I need you to tell me everything you know, from the beginning.

ARWEN: They were attacked several days ago, at Weathertop. Five of them, according to Aragorn.

ELROND: Five Nazgûl?

ARWEN: Yes. One of them managed to wound him before Aragorn drove them off with fire.

ELROND: And you weren't there when it happened?

ARWEN: No. I found the group afterward, while they were looking for athelas. By then the wound was already a couple of days old.

ELROND: I understand. Do you know exactly what wounded him?

ARWEN: Aragorn said it was a blade. Not an ordinary one.

ELROND: Did he tell you anything more about it? Did something break off inside?

ARWEN: That's what he feared. He wasn't certain.

ELROND: Very well, let's continue. What did Aragorn do for the wound, on the road?

ARWEN: He applied athelas the first night. And again the next day, I think.

ELROND: Did it help?

ARWEN: At first it seemed to. Afterward, not so much.

ELROND: When did he start getting worse, roughly?

ARWEN: Hard to say an exact day. I noticed him worse each time I looked at him. Paler, more tired.

ELROND: Was he still speaking clearly, by then?

ARWEN: Yes, most of the way. He faded little by little, until he stopped answering me, near the Ford.

ELROND: I need you to tell me exactly what he told you, in his own words if you remember them.

ARWEN: At first he only complained about the shoulder. Then he started saying stranger things.

ELROND: Like what?

ARWEN: That everything looked pale. Distant. "Like I'm fading," he told me, more than once.

ELROND: And the pain? Where exactly did he feel it?

ARWEN: It started in the shoulder, he said. But he told me he felt the cold moving from there toward his chest, a little more each day.

ELROND: Fever? Chills?

ARWEN: I asked him directly, the last time we spoke clearly. He told me he didn't think he had a fever.

ELROND: What did you notice yourself, apart from what he told you?

ARWEN: That he was wrong, or didn't realize. By the time we crossed the Ford he was burning to the touch, and shaking without stopping.

ELROND: Good, that's important. Anything else? Did he eat, drink water on the road?

ARWEN: Little, these last two days. He had no appetite or strength for much.

ELROND: And the arm? Did he ever move it in front of you?

ARWEN: Before he lost consciousness he told me it felt weaker than the other. That was one of the last clear things he said.

ELROND: Very well. Let me have a look at him.

ELROND: Temperature, thirty-eight point one degrees Celsius. Heart rate, one hundred two beats per minute. Respiratory rate, twenty breaths per minute. Blood pressure, one hundred over sixty-five millimeters of mercury. Oxygen saturation, ninety-six percent.

ELROND: Let me check his eyes a moment. Pupils are equal, reactive to light. That's something, at least.

ELROND: Heartbeat's steady. Lungs sound clear, if a little shallow.

ELROND: Now let me remove this bandage.

ELROND: The edge of the wound is pale. Almost white. Cold to the touch, even with the rest of him this warm from fever.

ARWEN: Is that normal?

ELROND: No. A common infection would turn red, swollen, warm around it. This is different. And here — look at this — these pale lines running up toward the chest, they shouldn't be there either.

ARWEN: What does it mean?

ELROND: That the blade left something behind that's still active. Something beyond what Aragorn could treat on the road, with what he had at hand.

ELROND: Let me try the arm now.

ELROND: Nothing. No response to stimulus at all.

ARWEN: Is that from the pain, or something more?

ELROND: I don't know for certain yet. It could be both. I'm going to clean the wound and immobilize the arm. After that, we can only watch how it develops over the next few hours.

ARWEN: And if he wakes?

ELROND: If he wakes, even for a moment, I want you to call me immediately. Whatever he says, however small it seems, I need to know it.

ARWEN: I'll stay with him.

ELROND: Good. I'll be back shortly with what's needed for the wound.
