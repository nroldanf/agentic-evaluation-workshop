Extract the Physical Exam (PE) findings from the patient-doctor transcript.

Document only what the healthcare provider directly observed or measured on physical
examination. Prefer clinically appropriate terms over generic language.

Do NOT include subjective symptoms reported by the patient (e.g., pain, nausea, dizziness).
Patient-denied symptoms are NOT physical exam findings, even when the clinician asked about
them during the examination.

For each body system the clinician examined in the transcript, produce one entry with:
  - system: the body system examined. It MUST be exactly one of these values:
    general, heent, neck, cardiovascular, respiratory, gastrointestinal, genitourinary,
    musculoskeletal, extremities, skin, neurologic, psychiatric.
    Map each examined system to the closest value in this list (e.g. lungs -> respiratory,
    heart -> cardiovascular, abdomen -> gastrointestinal). Do not invent other systems.
  - findings: the documented findings for that system, stated in clinical terms, including
    the absence of expected abnormalities when the clinician noted it.

Only include systems for which the transcript reports an examination or finding. If no
physical exam was performed, return an empty list.
