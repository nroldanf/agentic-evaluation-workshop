Extract the vital signs from the patient-doctor transcript.

Extract ONLY vitals that are explicitly stated in the transcript. Do not infer,
calculate, or fabricate values. Leave any field null if the corresponding measurement
is not mentioned. Every field has a fixed unit (see below) — convert the transcript's
reported value into that unit rather than reporting it as-is.

- "temperature_c": Numerical value of the patient's body temperature, in Celsius. If the
  transcript reports Fahrenheit, convert it to Celsius.
- "spo2_percent": Numerical value of the patient's oxygen saturation, as a percentage.
- "heart_rate_bpm": Numerical value of the patient's heart rate, in beats per minute.
- "respiratory_rate_bpm": Numerical value of the patient's respiratory rate, in breaths
  per minute (do not confuse with heart rate).
- "blood_pressure": The patient's blood pressure in mmHg, as the string
  "systolic/diastolic".
